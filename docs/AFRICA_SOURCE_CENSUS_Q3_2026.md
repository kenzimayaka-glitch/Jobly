# Africa Source Census — Q3 2026

This is the permanent quarterly control for Jobly's Africa offer collection.

## Objective

For every one of the 54 African countries, discover the maximum practical number of employment channels, then qualify each channel before it can feed Jobly.

A country is not considered "complete" merely because it has a pan-African source.

## Required channel families

1. Public employment services: employment agencies, labour ministries, national vacancy portals, public-service recruitment.
2. National private boards: general job boards, classifieds and local employment marketplaces.
3. Recruitment: staffing firms, headhunters and recruitment agencies with public vacancies.
4. Specialist boards: IT, engineering, health, finance, NGO/development, education, agriculture, mining/energy, construction, hospitality, logistics, telecoms and other material sectors.
5. Institutional: UN, development banks, NGOs, regional organisations and international programmes.
6. Graduate/university: university career centres, graduate portals, internship and graduate schemes.
7. Employer career surfaces: major local employers where they materially add jobs not present in other channels.
8. Pan-African and remote: continent-wide boards and Africa-eligible remote work.

## Qualification states

- DISCOVERED: candidate channel found.
- VERIFIED: URL/access and employment relevance confirmed.
- ACTIVE: current job surface confirmed and registered in Jobly.
- HARVESTED: Jobly successfully collected at least one offer.
- ACTIVE_EMPTY: source is live but currently has no usable offer.
- BLOCKED: source exists but collection is blocked.
- RETIRED: source no longer operates or no longer publishes relevant vacancies.

## Evidence policy

The census runner is a discovery instrument, not proof that every discovered domain is harvestable. Every candidate must subsequently pass a smoke crawl and an offer-level test.

## Quarterly delta

Each quarter compare the new report with the previous artifact and classify:
- new channel
- recovered channel
- blocked channel
- retired channel
- unchanged channel
- new collection method
- changed URL
- changed offer volume

The goal is cumulative source coverage, not a one-off list.
